Our Story

In 2006, five passionate runners began meeting at dawn in Nairobi's suburbs, bonding over shared miles and post-run breakfasts. What started as informal gatherings soon grew into something extraordinary.

"The name Urban Swaras emerged from our love for the city's energy ('Urban') and the Swahili word for antelope ('Swaras') - symbolizing our members' grace and endurance."

By 2011, we officially registered as a club, moving from home porches to restaurants. Today, we're 100+ strong, with members competing globally while keeping our grassroots spirit alive.
